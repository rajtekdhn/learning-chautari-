Grammar Rules

यी sample notes हुन् — तपाईं जति चाहनुहुन्छ topic थप्न वा edit गर्न सक्नुहुन्छ (तल HTML मा "acc-item" copy गरेर नयाँ थप्नुहोस्)।

Present Simple

Subject + V1 (s/es for 3rd person singular). Example: He goes to school every day.

दैनिक बानी वा सधैं हुने काम बताउन प्रयोग हुन्छ।

Past Simple

Subject + V2. Example: She visited Pokhara last year.

Future Simple

Subject + will/shall + V1. Example: They will attend the class tomorrow.

यी तीनवटा हुन् basic tense — perfect र continuous forms छिट्टै थपिनेछ।

a / an — unspecific, singular, countable nouns अगाडि प्रयोग हुन्छ। Consonant sound अगाडि a, vowel sound अगाडि an।

Example: a book, an apple, a university (sound "yu"), an hour (silent h)

the — specific वा already-known noun अगाडि प्रयोग हुन्छ। Example: The sun rises in the east.

in — महिना, वर्ष, ठूलो ठाउँको लागि। in 2026, in Kathmandu

on — दिन, मिति, सतह (surface) को लागि। on Monday, on the table

at — समय, सानो ठाउँको लागि। at 6 o'clock, at the gate

Active: Subject + Verb + Object. Ram wrote a letter.

Passive: Object + be + V3 + by + Subject. A letter was written by Ram.

Passive voice मा object सुरुमा आउँछ र verb को third form (V3) प्रयोग हुन्छ।

Positive sentence → negative tag; Negative sentence → positive tag।

He goes to school every day, doesn't he?

She isn't coming today, is she?

Grammar Exercises

1. She ______ (go) to school every day.

Answer: goes

2. Choose the correct article: I saw ______ elephant at the zoo.

Answer: an (elephant starts with a vowel sound)

3. Change to passive voice: "The teacher teaches the students."

Answer: The students are taught by the teacher.

4. Fill in the blank preposition: We will meet ______ 5 o'clock ______ the park.

Answer: at 5 o'clock, in the park

5. Add the correct tag: You like tea, ______?

Answer: don't you
